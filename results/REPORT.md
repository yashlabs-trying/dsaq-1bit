## Answers

**Q1: Can we reliably detect blocks where DSA is worth using? NO under the preregistered practical rule (held-out Spearman > 0.30 and balanced accuracy > 0.60).**

**Q2: Does seeded search justify itself on DSA failures? NO under the preregistered rule (wins on >50% of held-out irregular blocks and lowers weighted aggregate error).**

These answers apply only to the tested DSA surfaces, distributions, model, block size, and representative layers.
